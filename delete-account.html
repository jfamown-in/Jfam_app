Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your JFAM Fresh Dairy account directly inside the mobile app or by submitting a deletion request below.

Option 1: Delete directly in the JFAM app

  1. Open the JFAM app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Permanently Delete Account.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from your registered email address with the subject "JFAM Account Deletion Request" and include your registered phone number. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Email Deletion Request (jfamown@gmail.com)

What is deleted vs. retained
  • Deleted immediately: Your JFAM login account, profile details (name, email, mobile number, date of birth), saved delivery addresses, delivery map coordinates, wishlist, and active cart.
  • Retained where required by law: Anonymized historical order and invoice records may be retained for up to 3 years solely for tax, accounting, and dispute-resolution compliance, with personal contact details removed.
